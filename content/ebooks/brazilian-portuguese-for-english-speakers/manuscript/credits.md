# Credits and Notices

## How this book was made

This book was drafted with the help of artificial intelligence, and its illustrations are AI-generated in a didactic drawing style. The factual claims were tied to the research file kept with the manuscript, and no human review of the full text is declared here. No native-speaker check was performed on the Portuguese examples, respellings or transcriptions, so every example that is not tied to a cited source is the author's illustration and remains unconfirmed.

The illustrations are didactic drawings. They do not depict real people, places or events.

## Title

Brazilian Portuguese for English Speakers: A Strategy Guide for False Beginners (working title). The title is provisional and may change before release.

## Variant

This book teaches Brazilian Portuguese. The Celpe-Bras exam is used only as a reference for the Brazilian variant [F7]. The book makes no promise about preparation for that exam or for any other.

## Sources

The factual claims in the chapters point to numbered sources, written as [F1], [F2] and so on, which are listed in the research file kept with the manuscript. A claim without a number is the author's suggestion or the author's illustration, and the text says so where it matters.

## What is not stated here

The author name, the publisher and the edition date are not stated in this file. They will be added when they are decided.
