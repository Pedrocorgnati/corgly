# Images plan for the manuscript (companion to outline.md)

- Style restriction: every image is an evident didactic drawing, diagram, chart or labelled sketch made for teaching, never a photo or a realistic rendering.
- No photorealistic image of a real person, place or event presented as a photo: characters, mouths, streets and scenes are simple, clearly drawn illustrations with visible outlines and limited color.
- No photorealistic likeness of any real speaker, politician, celebrity or teacher; faces are generic and stylized, and no real logo or trademark appears.
- No stock photo, no photo composite and no AI photo of a real place presented as documentation; a map or a mouth cross-section must look drawn and labelled.
- Text inside images is limited to Portuguese words, respelling and short English labels, following the outline notation policy: broad IPA only in the figures of chapters 3 and 4 and in the appendix minimal-pairs figure.
- All drawings are shared-deck friendly: dark outline on light background, one idea per image, labels readable at PDF page width.
- Aspect ratios below are suggestions for the layout; no image is generated at this step.
